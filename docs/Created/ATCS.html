1. OCTG Inspection and Maintenance Services

Our aim is to serve oil companies by providing timely, high-quality, cost-effective Tubular Inspection Services locally. We have a highly experienced Panel of Inspectors who can be deputed within a short time for the Inspection of Tubulars, BHA, and drill string components.

Drill Pipe Inspection

We provide a full range of pipe inspection sizes ranging from 2-3/8" to 6.5/8" while following customer's specifications and edition of DS-1 or API guidelines. We have state-of-the-art tube inspection equipment to meet API inspection requirements or third-party inspections, including full-length wall thickness measurement of pipe tubes.

Services include:

  • Magnetic particle inspections on tool joints and upsets
  • EMI inspection
  • Visual Inspection and Thread gauging
  • Dimensional Check
  • Refacing services
  • Straightening services
  • Hydro blasting Internal & External
  • Cement Removal services

Tubing & Casing Inspection

Depending on client's requirements, the general scope or part of the scope may be covered. Our experienced inspectors follow industry standard guidelines and documented Standard Operating Procedures.

Services include:

  • API full length drifting
  • FLMPI on Casings
  • End area Inspections
  • Gauging services
  • Wall thickness measurements
  • Maintenance services
  • Hydro jetting services

Bottom Hole Assembly Inspection

BHA inspections are carried out as per latest editions of API and DS-1 (3-5) guidelines.

Services include:

  • Black light inspection of end areas
  • MPI on upsets and wear pads
  • Visual inspection and thread gauging
  • Refacing services
  • Straightening services for Drill Pipe and HWDP
  • Liquid dye penetrant inspections

We have fleets of BHA Inspection units available in our tubular goods Inspection facility that can also be moved to clients' Onshore & Offshore facilities.

Drilling Handling Tools & CAT IV Inspection

  • Dismantling, blasting, inspection, reassembling, repainting, and maintenance of drilling tools such as elevators, slips, and spiders

Additional Services

  • Drill Pipe Straightening – Correcting bent or bowed drill pipes to ensure optimal performance
  • Refacing – Reconditioning worn tool joints to restore original dimensions and efficiency
  • Thread Inspection and Repair – Inspecting and restoring damaged or worn threads on OCTG components
  • Non-Destructive Testing (NDT) – Includes ultrasonic, magnetic particle, and eddy current testing to detect internal and external flaws
  • Water Blasting – Internal and external cleaning for all types of downhole tools (e.g., drill pipe, BHA, tubing, casing)

2. Corrosion Control Services

Aries Corrosion Control & Management services is molded out by a group of corrosion experts to deliver prompt solutions for extending the life of structural elements, metal/non-metal, doctored by proper analysis and treatment.

Offshore/Marine structures are built irrespective of the atmospheric conditions to which they are exposed. Hence, proper coating procedure is crucial for any offshore/marine structure for preserving and maintaining it as good as new. This relies on procedures which include proper surface treatment techniques, required coating materials and methods of applications.

4. Load Testing, Wire Rope Socketing & Spooling Solutions

At Aries Group, Qatar, we deliver high-quality Load Testing, Wire Rope Socketing, and Wire Rope Spooling services, backed by Aries Group's extensive global expertise. Our services are tailored to meet the needs of the marine, oil & gas, construction, and industrial sectors, with strict adherence to international and regional standards.

Load Testing Services

We offer complete load testing solutions for all types of lifting gears and equipment, available both onsite and at our dedicated testing yard in Birkat Al Awamir.

Our capabilities include:

  • Load Testing Blocks up to 200 tons
  • Testing Tray with capacity up to 120 tons
  • Tensile Test Bed capacity up to 100 tons for destructive and non-destructive testing
  • Sample destruction testing of lifting gears in compliance with Qatar Energy lifting regulations
  • Proof load testing, Functional and dynamic testing
  • Third-party witness certification available upon request

We ensure every test is completed safely, accurately, and in full compliance with LEEA, LOLER, API, and Qatar Energy requirements.

Wire Rope Socketing Services

Our professional wire rope socketing services use high-grade resin (pour socketing) and mechanical methods to create reliable and high-strength terminations, including:

  • Full preparation and cleaning of wire ropes before socketing
  • Casting and curing with certified resins
  • Post-socketing inspection and proof load testing
  • Compliance with offshore and heavy lifting standards (API, DNV, ABS)

Each assembly undergoes strict quality control to ensure maximum strength, safety, and durability.

Wire Rope Spooling Services

We provide expert wire rope spooling services for cranes, winches, hoists, and other critical equipment.

Our specialized spooling operations include:

  • Controlled tension spooling to prevent rope damage
  • Uniform layering to minimize wear and extend service life
  • Handling a wide range of rope diameters and types
  • Onsite and offsite spooling support across Qatar

Our precision techniques ensure reliable rope performance and reduced maintenance costs.
